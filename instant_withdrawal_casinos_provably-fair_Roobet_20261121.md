## RTP and Provably Fair Gaming Explained

Return to player (RTP) is the percentage of all wagered money a game returns to players over time. A slot with a 97% RTP theoretically returns $97 for every $100 wagered across millions of spins. Higher RTP means a smaller house edge, so seek out games in the 96–99% range whenever possible.

Provably fair gaming takes transparency a step further. Each bet is tied to a cryptographic seed that you can verify after the fact. The casino commits to a hashed seed before you play, then reveals it afterward so you can confirm the outcome was not manipulated. This is the defining feature of the provably-fair niche, and it pairs naturally with instant payouts: both put control back in the player's hands.
