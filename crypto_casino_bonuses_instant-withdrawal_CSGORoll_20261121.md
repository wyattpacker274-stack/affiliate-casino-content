### Key Terms: Wagering Requirements, Max Cashout, and Eligible Games

Before claiming any crypto casino bonus, you should understand the fine print:

*   **Eligible games:** Not all games contribute equally to wagering. Slots often contribute 100%, while table games and live dealer options may contribute 10% or less.
*   **Expiry:** Bonuses usually expire within 7 to 30 days if unused.

## Types of Crypto Casino Bonuses Explained

Understanding the different bonus formats helps you choose the offer that matches your playing style and withdrawal preferences.

### Welcome Deposit Match

The most common crypto casino bonus. The casino matches your first deposit by a certain percentage (e.g., 100% up to 1 BTC). These bonuses usually carry wagering requirements but provide the largest bankroll boost.

### No Deposit Bonus

A small amount of crypto or free spins awarded just for registering. These are rare but valuable because they let you test the platform without risking your own funds. Withdrawal limits are usually strict.

### Free Spins

Often bundled with welcome packages or offered as standalone promotions. Free spins are typically tied to specific slot games. Winnings from free spins may be subject to wagering requirements before instant withdrawal.

Reload bonuses reward existing players on subsequent deposits. Cashback returns a percentage of losses over a set period (daily, weekly, or monthly). Both are ideal for players who plan to stay at one casino long-term.

### Loyalty and VIP Rewards

Top instant-withdrawal casinos run VIP programs where you earn points for every wager. Points can be exchanged for cash, free spins, or higher withdrawal limits. High-volume players often receive personal account managers and faster payout processing.
