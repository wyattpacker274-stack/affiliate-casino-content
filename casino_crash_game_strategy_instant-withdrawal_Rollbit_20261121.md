### The Martingale Variant
The Martingale system involves doubling your bet after each loss, with the goal of recouping all previous losses plus a profit equal to the original stake upon a win. In crash, this can be adapted by increasing your bet size after losses and resetting to the base amount after a cashout. While this can work in the short term, a prolonged crash streak can quickly exceed table limits or deplete your bankroll. Use this only with a substantial bankroll and strict stop-loss boundaries.

### The d'Alembert Approach
A more conservative alternative, the d'Alembert system increases your bet by one unit after a loss and decreases it by one unit after a win. This spreads risk more evenly and is often preferred by players who want steady progression without the exponential bet growth of Martingale.

### Flat Betting
Flat betting—wagering the same amount on every round—is the simplest strategy and aligns well with bankroll management principles. It’s ideal for players who prioritize longevity over aggressive growth and who want to test the game’s rhythm without complex progression rules.
