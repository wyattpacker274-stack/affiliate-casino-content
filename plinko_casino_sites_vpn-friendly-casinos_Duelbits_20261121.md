## What Are Plinko Casino Sites?

Plinko casino sites are online gambling platforms that host the drop-ball game popularized by *The Price Is Right* and later adapted into a provably fair casino format. Instead of physical pegs, players drop a ball down a triangular board of pegs, and the ball lands in a multiplier slot at the bottom. The house edge is baked into the multiplier distribution, and the risk level (low, medium, high) changes both the volatility and the payout spread.

The modern crypto version — pioneered by Stake and now replicated across dozens of platforms — uses a provably fair algorithm. That means every drop can be verified against a server seed, client seed, and nonce. For players who care about transparency, this is a major draw.

### How Plinko Works

- **Choose your bet size** and the number of rows (typically 8 to 16).
- **Select a risk level**: low risk clusters multipliers near 1x, while high risk pushes toward rare 1000x+ outcomes.
- **Drop the ball** and watch it bounce through the peg pyramid.
- **Verify the result** using the provably fair hash if you want to confirm the outcome wasn't manipulated.
