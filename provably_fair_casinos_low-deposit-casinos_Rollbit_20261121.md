## What Are Provably Fair Casinos?

Provably fair casinos are online gambling sites that use a cryptographic commitment scheme — typically SHA-256 hashing — to prove that game outcomes were fixed before the round began and could not be manipulated by the operator or the player.

The concept originated with Bitcoin dice sites around 2013 and has since become the default standard across crypto casinos. Traditional online casinos rely on third-party RNG audits (eCOGRA, iTech Labs, GLI), which are periodic and retrospective. Provably fair gambling is different: verification is **per-bet and instant**, and you don't need to trust an auditor — you can check the math yourself.

### How the Provably Fair Algorithm Works

Most provably fair games use a three-part seed system:

1. **Server seed** — generated by the casino. Before you play, the casino shows you a hashed (encrypted) version of it.
2. **Client seed** — generated by your browser, and editable by you. This is your contribution to the randomness.
3. **Nonce** — a counter that increments with every bet, ensuring each round produces a unique result.

The casino combines these three inputs, runs them through a hashing function (usually HMAC-SHA256), and converts the resulting hexadecimal string into a game outcome — a dice roll, a crash multiplier, a card order.

### Why the Hash Matters

The critical step is that the casino publishes `hash(server_seed)` **before** you bet. Because a hash is a one-way function, the casino is locked in: it cannot change the server seed later without breaking the hash. Once you're done playing, the casino reveals the original server seed. You hash it yourself, confirm it matches the pre-committed value, and then re-run the outcome calculation with your client seed and nonce. If the numbers match what you experienced, the game was fair.

### Verifying a Bet in Practice

Every reputable provably fair casino ships a built-in verifier page, and most also support third-party open-source verifiers on GitHub. The typical workflow is:

- Copy the revealed server seed, your client seed, and the nonce.
- Paste them into the verifier.
- Compare the output to the actual result you received.

If you rotate your client seed regularly (recommended every session), you also prevent the casino from pre-computing outcomes against a known seed.
