## What Are Casino Crash Games?

Crash games are a genre of crypto casino games where a multiplier starts at 1.00x and climbs in real time. Players place a bet before the round begins and must cash out before the graph “crashes.” If you cash out at 2.50x, your stake is multiplied by 2.50. If the game crashes at 1.85x and you have not cashed out, you lose the entire wager.

The appeal lies in the simplicity and transparency. Most reputable crash games use a provably fair algorithm, allowing players to verify that each crash point was determined before the round started and was not manipulated by the operator. This transparency is especially important for VPN users who may be playing from jurisdictions with less regulatory oversight.

Some players apply a Martingale progression—doubling the bet after each loss—to crash games. While this can recover losses quickly during a short losing streak, it is dangerous in crash games because a long sequence of sub-2.00x crashes can exhaust a bankroll before a win occurs.

An anti-Martingale approach, where you increase bets after wins and decrease them after losses, is generally considered safer for crash gambling. It capitalizes on hot streaks while preserving capital during cold runs.
